# Plushbound Legends — Third-Party Notices

This document is the canonical notice register for third-party material distributed with Plushbound Legends.

A third-party component must not be marked release-ready unless its license/permission terms are known, compatible with the intended distribution, and any required notices are recorded here or in an attached license file.

## How to Add an Entry

For each third-party component, record:

- Name
- Version or revision, if applicable
- Author / copyright holder
- Source URL or source reference
- License name
- Full license text location or required notice
- Whether attribution is required in-game, in documentation, or both
- Whether the material was modified
- Any redistribution, commercial-use, share-alike, copyleft, trademark, patent, or other special conditions

## Included Third-Party Components

No third-party production asset is currently approved for final distribution unless separately recorded below.

## Important Rules

- A download being free does not automatically make it free to redistribute in a game.
- “Royalty-free” does not necessarily mean attribution-free, transferable, sublicensable, or usable in every commercial context.
- Store marketplace licenses, stock-site licenses, font licenses, and commissioned-work agreements should be retained with the project records.
- Copyleft/share-alike licenses require case-by-case review before integration because their obligations may affect distribution.
- Do not copy assets from commercial games, RPG Maker RTP, anime, films, websites, or other copyrighted sources without a license that permits the intended use.
- Trademark references and brand names should not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation unless authorized.
