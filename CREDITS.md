## Tools & Technology

## AI-Assisted Production

AI-assisted tools may be used during development for ideation, drafting, code assistance, image generation, audio experimentation, cleanup, or production support. AI assistance is not a substitute for tracking the human contributor responsible for selecting, editing, integrating, and approving production material. Specific production assets should record applicable tool/provenance details in the asset provenance registry.

## Credit Policy

- Credit people for the work they actually performed.
- Do not move substantial contributors into “Special Thanks” merely to shorten credits.
- Use the contributor's requested professional name when known and lawful to do so.
- Do not list a company or tool as the sole credit when individual contributors are contractually entitled to personal credit.
- Keep third-party license attribution separate from creative/development credits when practical.
- Do not publish private personal information in credits.
- Credit corrections should be treated as normal maintenance and fixed promptly.

## Special Thanks

Reserved for meaningful support that does not fit a production discipline. This section should not replace earned development credits.
